Research

Four places I've pointed the same idea

Every project below is a version of one question: what has to be true for a system of language models to be trusted with real work? Different domains stress different answers — pedagogy, scientific reasoning, device physics, safety.

Multi-agent orchestration for scientific research

SciWeaver

Four specialist agents coordinated over a DAG, with context passed adaptively between them, reasoning conditioned on a user profile, and Reflexion-style self-healing when a step fails. Scored by an LLM-as-a-judge framework across five dimensions.

  • +17.2% weighted score over GPT-5 and Gemini baselines
  • Validated with paired Wilcoxon tests and bootstrap CIs
  • Failure-mode engine catalogues where the pipeline breaks

An AI tutor that models what the student actually knows

SAGE

Six role-specialized agents over a hybrid Neo4j knowledge graph and vector retrieval pipeline, sequencing prerequisites toward a goal the learner sets. Mastery is tracked with Bayesian Knowledge Tracing and scheduled with spaced repetition; a deterministic gatekeeper enforces pedagogical guardrails before anything reaches the student.

  • Three-part memory: working, episodic, priority-scheduled review
  • CPU-local models for real-time intent classification
  • Published at IEEE ICHMS 2026

Reading device structure out of electrical measurements

Semiconductors & materials

An inverse-modeling framework for β-Ga2O3 MOSFETs that recovers internal geometry from I–V curves — the measurement is cheap, the physical characterization is not. A best-per-target ensemble picks the right estimator per dimension, and an agreement gate abstains when the models disagree.

  • R² = 0.990, 2.68% MAPE on held-out devices
  • 1-D CNN, gradient boosting, and a contrastive gate-position module
  • Related: closed-loop CNT growth guided by retrieval and human feedback

Agents that teach through a live environment

Cybersecurity training

A training platform where a reinforcement learning agent works alongside the learner inside the exercise, paired with a retrieval-grounded assistant that answers in under 1.4 seconds so guidance arrives while the task is still in the learner's head.

  • PPO agent, 100% objective completion rate
  • Perfect retrieval and safety scores on the assistant benchmark
